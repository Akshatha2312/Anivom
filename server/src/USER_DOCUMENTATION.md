# ANIVOM User & Admin Guide

## Customer User Guide

### 1. Account Management
- **Registration**: Click "Account" → "Sign Up". Provide your name, email, and password. You will automatically receive a unique referral code.
- **Login / Logout**: Access your profile using credentials or "Sign in with Google". Logout anytime from the account header.

### 2. Browsing & Product Selection
- **Catalog Navigation**: Filter products by Category, Size, Colour, and Price range slider. Search products by keyword or sort by price/newest.
- **Product Details**: Select desired T-shirt color and size. View high-resolution garment mockups for front/back views.

### 3. T-Shirt Customization Studio
- **Accessing Studio**: Click "Customize T-Shirt" from any product page or navigation header.
- **Adding Text**: Select "Add Text", type custom text, choose font family, size, alignment, and color.
- **Adding Predefined Designs**: Choose from curated SVG graphics (Graphics, Typography, Badges).
- **Uploading Image**: Upload custom image files (PNG/JPG/WEBP, max 5MB).
- **Manipulating Layers**: Drag elements to position, scale using control handles, or switch between Front and Back views.
- **Saving & Ordering**: Click "Add Customised Product to Bag" to send the customized garment directly to your cart.

### 4. Cart, Checkout & Payment
- **Cart Management**: Review items, adjust quantities, or apply discount coupons.
- **Checkout**: Select or create a delivery address. Review subtotal, discount, and grand total.
- **Payment**: Complete payment via Razorpay (UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, NetBanking).
- **Order Tracking**: Track order status (`PLACED`, `CONFIRMED`, `PROCESSING`, `SHIPPED`, `DELIVERED`) and request returns or cancellations from your account dashboard.

---

## Admin Panel Guide

### 1. Dashboard & Analytics
- View total sales revenue, active order count, customer statistics, and recent orders summary.

### 2. Product & Inventory Management
- Add new T-shirt products with multi-view garment images and price points.
- Manage variant stock levels across sizes (XS-XXXL) and colors. Toggle product visibility.

### 3. Order Processing & Status Updates
- View detailed customer order items, shipping snapshots, and customization designs.
- Update order statuses (`CONFIRMED`, `PROCESSING`, `SHIPPED`, `DELIVERED`, `CANCELLED`). Process return requests and manage refunds.

### 4. Banners, Coupons & Master Data
- Configure homepage hero banners, active promotional discount coupons, custom categories, sizes, and colors.
